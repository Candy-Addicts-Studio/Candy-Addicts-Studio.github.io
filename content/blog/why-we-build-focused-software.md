Candy Addicts Studio is a one-person software company. That naturally limits how many things can be built at once, but it also creates a useful constraint: every product needs a clear reason to exist.

The goal is not to fill a roadmap. It is to make focused tools, improve them deliberately, and share the decisions behind the work.

## Focus is a feature

Small products are easier to explain and easier to learn. When the purpose is clear, the interface can spend less time describing itself and more time helping someone complete the task they came for.

That means asking a few questions early:

- What is the single job this product should do well?
- Which options genuinely help that job?
- What can stay out until there is evidence it belongs?

> Useful software does not need to look busy. It needs to make the next step obvious.

## Shipping with context

A product page can show what exists. A build note can explain why it exists, what changed, and what tradeoffs shaped the result. That context helps people decide whether a tool fits their workflow—and gives future development a record to build from.

This blog will collect those notes: product updates, implementation lessons, and practical decisions from building software in public.
